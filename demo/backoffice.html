A new tenant starts cash only. Only cash reaches the drawer. Every other method records an amount and authorises nothing — no gateway, no QR, no settlement. Code branches on kind, never on a name.
Why VAT ships off
Most carinderias sit below the ₱3,000,000 registration threshold and are not VAT-registered.

Shipping VAT on by default would hand those tenants figures that are confidently wrong, and a receipt implying a registration they do not hold.

So: off, until an owner says otherwise.

Cash is seeded at tenant creation and is undeletable — enforced by a database constraint. The Discount list is not here; it is back-office CRUD alongside the catalog.
